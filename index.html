Troubleshooting

Use Help Center search with keywords such as holdings, documents, activity, tax, video or chat. If no results appear, try a broader term or use Chat Support for immediate guidance.
When a resource cannot be accessed, the Help Materials section shows a clear unavailable-resource message instead of a broken link. You can try again later or contact support for assistance.
The Help Center is responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile. On smaller screens, content stacks vertically so you can access categories, videos, downloads and chat more easily.
