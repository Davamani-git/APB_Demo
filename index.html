How do I access the Help Center?−

Select the Help Center option in the Home Page navigation to open this support area.

Can I search for a help topic?+

Yes. Enter keywords in the Help Center search bar to locate matching support topics, FAQs, guides and materials.

Does Help Center work on mobile devices?+

Yes. The Help Center layout is responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.

What if a help resource is unavailable?+

If a resource is unavailable, the page presents a clear message instead of a broken link or blank area.

How to explore product guidance

Open a section, review the article text, and follow the steps presented under each help topic.

  1. Choose a category such as FAQs or Troubleshooting.
  2. Read the article summary and supporting instructions.
  3. Use search when you know a keyword but not the category.

How to choose the right support option

Use text for quick reading, videos for demonstrations, materials for downloadable reference, and chat for guided assistance.
